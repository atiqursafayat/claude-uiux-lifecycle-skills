# Interview guide: <topic>

- **Goal:** what we need to learn, and which decision it informs
- **Participants:** role or segment, how many (5–8 per role), recruiting source, incentives
- **Consent:** recording? how notes are stored and anonymised

## Intro (2 min)
Thanks. We're improving <product> and want to learn from your experience. There are no wrong answers; we're testing our product, not you. May I take notes or record?

## Context (5 min)
- Tell me about your role and how <topic> fits into your week.

## Past behaviour (20 min). Ask for stories, not opinions.
- Tell me about the last time you <did the task>. Walk me through it.
- What happened next? Who else was involved?
- What was hardest or most annoying? What did you do about it?
- What tools, workarounds or people did you rely on?
- Have you ever given up partway? What happened?

## Wrap-up (3 min)
- If you could change one thing about <task>, what would it be?
- Is there anything I should have asked?

## Interviewer rules
- Don't pitch or explain the product; ask "why?" and "can you show me?"
- Avoid leading and hypothetical questions ("Would you use…?").
- Note what they *do*, not just what they say.
